# Template — The Build Log

> **Use for:** You built or shipped something. A Skill, a video, an automation, a system. Showing the process is the artifact.
> **Target length:** 1,000-1,500 words.
> **Voice:** Field-log. Declarative. Show the work, don't pitch the work.

---

## The structure

Hook → Why → The build → What broke → Where this goes → Artifact callout → CTA.

The "what broke" section is optional but the highest-trust move you can make. Readers who recognize the failure modes feel seen; readers who don't get a preview of what to watch for.

## What makes this template work

1. **The hook is concrete, not abstract.** Opens with the moment or the result. Specifics earn the click; abstractions kill it.
2. **"Why I built it" prevents the post from feeling like a feature demo.** Naming the actual friction (a forecast call gone wrong, a deadline missed, a question asked for the fifth time) signals operator-to-operator.
3. **The build is numbered.** Steps are bulleted, one action each. No step-stacking. A reader should be able to recreate the work from the post alone.
4. **"What broke" is the trust accelerator.** Most build content shows the polished result. Showing the failure modes is what makes the post feel real.
5. **"Where this goes" closes the loop.** Connects the specific build to the larger frame so the post earns its place in your body of work, not just as a one-off.

## Voice notes

- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- One bolded line per section, sparingly
- Em dashes are fine — they're field-log voice, not AI-tells
- Avoid: "let's dive in," hype vocabulary (unlock, transform, leverage, supercharge, game-changing), "buckle up"
- Replace "use" not "utilize," "strong" not "robust," "shipped" not "delivered"
